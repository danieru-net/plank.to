# Store listing rules

The listing copy for both stores (name, subtitle, promotional text, keywords, descriptions, what's new, URLs) lives in the [Store listings section of the README](../../README.md#store-listings). This file holds the rules that copy must follow.

Rules: no clinical, therapeutic or efficacy claims (no "helps ADHD/anxiety", "improves focus", "proven"). Weather is optional and described that way. Do not say data "never leaves your device" (see [../backups.md](../backups.md)). Weather suggestions are informational, not health or safety advice.

## Before submitting

- Check the name "Plank" is available in each store (it may be taken; fall back to "Plank Planner"). `[[PLACEHOLDER: name availability confirmed]]`
- Confirm the exact Open-Meteo attribution wording (plan.md 4.1/4.9). `[[PLACEHOLDER]]`
- Confirm https://plank.to/privacy and https://plank.to/contact are live before using them as store URLs.
- iOS keywords: do not repeat the app name or add competitor names.

## Content rating

Play content rating questionnaire: no user-generated content shared, no ads, no purchases, no location sharing with other users.

## Claims review

Avoid: "treats", "therapy", "clinically", "reduces stress", "improves focus", "helps with ADHD/autism/anxiety", or labels that read as a medical product ("ADHD app", "autism therapy"). Naming who the app is made for ("made for autistic people, people with ADHD, and anyone who wants a clear, predictable day") is fine. Weather/clothing: avoid "keeps you safe", "prevents illness". Co-design is not claimed unless 6.10 sessions have happened.
