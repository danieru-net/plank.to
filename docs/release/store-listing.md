## Full description (both stores, under 4000 chars)

Plank is a calm daily planner for tasks and weekly routines, made for autistic people, people with ADHD, and anyone who wants a clear, predictable day. It works without an account and without a connection.

Today
- See what is planned for today in one clear list.
- Add a task with an optional time, notes, and picture.
- Mark things done, move them, or dismiss them without fuss.

Routines
- Repeat something every day or on the weekdays you choose, at a local time you pick.
- Change or skip one day without silently changing the whole routine. You choose whether an edit affects one occurrence or the series.

Reminders, only if you want them
- Reminders are local notifications on your device. They are off until you turn them on.
- No streaks, no scores, no pressure, and no repeated nagging.

Weather and clothing ideas, optional
- Weather is off by default. If you turn it on, you can use your approximate location or search for a city or postcode instead.
- Plank shows a short forecast and a clothing idea based on the clothing choices you set yourself. Suggestions are informational only and you can dismiss them.
- Turning weather off removes the saved location and forecast from the app.
- Forecast data by Open-Meteo.com. `[[PLACEHOLDER: confirm exact attribution wording, plan.md 4.1/4.9]]`

Made to be adjustable
- Choose text size, density, and color theme.
- No ads, no analytics, no accounts, no subscriptions.

Privacy
- Your tasks and routines are stored on your device. Plank does not send them anywhere. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include a copy, depending on your settings.
- Privacy policy: `[[PLACEHOLDER: https://plank.to/privacy once deployed]]`
